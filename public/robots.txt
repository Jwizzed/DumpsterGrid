# Do NOT Disallow the "estimated" city pages here. Those pages carry
# <meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow"> instead (see [slug].astro,
# INDEX_ESTIMATED_PAGES). A robots.txt Disallow stops crawlers from fetching
# the page at all, which means Googlebot never reads the noindex tag and the
# URL can still surface in results as an uncrawled listing. Allow + noindex
# is the correct combination: Google fetches the page, honors noindex, and
# the internal links on it still pass crawl equity to the verified pages.
